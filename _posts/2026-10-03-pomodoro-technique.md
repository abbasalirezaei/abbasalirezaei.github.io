Focusing has become the hardest thing in the world these days...

It's a problem almost all of us struggle with: difficulty focusing on a single topic, or losing focus altogether.

Well, I've been using a method called **Pomodoro** for about **8 years** now (since my university entrance exam days), and it has helped me a lot with staying focused. I should also mention that I've tried many different methods, but none of them worked as well as this one.

It doesn't matter whether you want to study a language, do programming, cook, or get through household chores — I **guarantee** this method can help you.

In fact, when you dig into this method, most of the key principles of planning are already built into it:
- Rewarding yourself after completing a task
- Breaking work down into smaller tasks
- And more...

Well, I'll share the story behind this technique at the end of this post, because I want to get straight to the point for those who don't have much time...

## The Pomodoro Technique

It's a **time management method** where you work in **25-minute intervals** (called a Pomodoro) and take **5-minute breaks** in between.

As for why 25 minutes and not 26, I've covered that in the story at the end of the post.

What makes this method so lovable:
- ✅ It's incredibly simple
- ✅ It dramatically boosts focus
- ✅ It minimizes distractions

## How to Do It

1. Pick a specific task
2. Set a 25-minute timer
3. Focus only on that task until the timer goes off
4. Take a 5-minute break
5. Repeat the cycle
6. After every 4 rounds, take a longer break (**15 to 30 minutes**)

## An Even Simpler Way

Some websites make this incredibly easy. They run the cycle for you, count and save your tomatoes, let you specify how many tomatoes you want to spend on each task, and show you your stats at the end of the day or week.

Recently, I noticed **Windows 11** has also added this feature — you can access it from the `timer and clock` section.

But in my opinion, don't get caught up in which app or platform to use. I've used two different websites and both were great. Use whichever you like and enjoy it. 😊
